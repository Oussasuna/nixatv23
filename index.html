QUESTIONS? ANSWERS

What is NIXATV?

NIXATV is a premium subscription service providing live TV, movies, and series over the internet. You can watch it on almost any smart device without the need for traditional cables.

What Internet speed do I need?

We recommend a minimum of 15 Mbps for standard streaming, and at least 30 Mbps if you want to enjoy our 4K channels and movies without any interruptions.

Do I need a VPN?

A VPN is not strictly required as our servers are highly secure. However, if your Internet Service Provider (ISP) is blocking or throttling traffic, using a VPN will solve the issue entirely.

How do I activate my subscription?

Simply choose a plan and message us on WhatsApp. Once payment is confirmed, we will instantly send you your login credentials and a simple step-by-step guide to get started on your specific device.

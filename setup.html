SETUP GUIDES

Follow these simple tutorials to set up NIXATV on your preferred device.

SELECT YOUR DEVICE

AMAZON FIRESTICK

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Go to Main Menu > Settings > My Fire TV.

  • 2

    Select "Developer Options" and turn on "Apps from Unknown Sources".

  • 3

    Go back to home, search for "Downloader" app and install it.

  • 4

    Open Downloader and enter this URL: http://iptvsmarters.com/smarters.apk

  • 5

    Click Go, wait for download, then click Install.

  • 6

    Open the app, select "Login with Xtream Codes API".

  • 7

    Enter the details received in your email (Username, Password, URL).

ANDROID TV / BOX

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Go to the Google Play Store on your Android TV or Box.

  • 2

    Search for the app IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate.

  • 3

    Click Install and wait for the application to download.

  • 4

    Open the app and select "Add New User" or "Login with Xtream Codes API".

  • 5

    Enter your assigned Name, Username, Password, and Server URL provided by us.

  • 6

    Click "Add User" and you will be logged in to enjoy your live channels and VODs.

SMART TV (LG/SAMSUNG)

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Go to the App Store on your LG or Samsung Smart TV.

  • 2

    Search for an IPTV application like IBO Player or Flix IPTV.

  • 3

    Install and open the application.

  • 4

    The app will display your TV's MAC Address and Device Key on the screen.

  • 5

    Send us a message on WhatsApp with your MAC Address, or upload the M3U playlist link we gave you on the app's website.

  • 6

    Restart your TV app and the channels will load automatically.

APPLE (IOS / APPLE TV)

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Open the Apple App Store on your device.

  • 2

    Search for Smarters Player Lite or GSE Smart IPTV.

  • 3

    Download and install the app.

  • 4

    Open the app and accept any terms and conditions.

  • 5

    Select "Login with Xtream Codes API".

  • 6

    Enter the Username, Password, and URL that we sent to your email.

PC / MACBOOK

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Download VLC Media Player from their official website (videolan.org).

  • 2

    Install and open VLC Media Player on your PC or Mac.

  • 3

    Go to "Media" > "Open Network Stream" in the top menu.

  • 4

    Paste the M3U Playlist URL you received from us into the network URL field.

  • 5

    Click "Play" and wait a few seconds for the channel list to load.

  • 6

    Alternatively, you can download the IPTV Smarters app for Windows/Mac.

MAG BOX / STB

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
  • 1

    Find your MAC Address on the sticker under your MAG Box (Format: 00:1A:79:XX:XX:XX).

  • 2

    Send us this MAC Address on WhatsApp so we can activate it on our server.

  • 3

    Turn on your MAG device and go to Settings > System Settings.

  • 4

    Go to Servers > Portals.

  • 5

    Set Portal 1 Name as "NIXATV" and enter the Portal URL we gave you.

  • 6

    Click OK to save, return to the main menu, and reboot the portal.
